# Focus – User guide

Focus helps you focus calmly: start a session, take breaks in the Pomodoro
rhythm and let distracting apps rest meanwhile. Everything stays on your phone.

## Install

1. On your phone, download
   [`focus_timer.apk`](https://github.com/maestroDev3/Focus/releases/download/latest/focus_timer.apk)
   and open it. Allow your browser to install apps if Android asks.
2. Updates: download the new APK and install it over the old one – your data stays.
3. **Restricted settings (one time):** Android greys out the accessibility and
   notification switches for apps from outside the Play Store. Open
   *Settings → Apps → Focus*, tap **⋮** and choose **Allow restricted
   settings**. Focus shows this hint and an “Open app info” button when needed.

## First steps

The home screen shows the date, a greeting and the **ring** with today's focus
time against your daily goal. Below it are chips for the **session length**,
the **label**, the **sound** and the **paused apps**, and the line
“What are you working on?” for an optional intention.

**Tap the ring** – your session begins.

## Sessions and breaks

- The session screen counts down calmly. You can pause and resume; leaving
  Focus does **not** end the session.
- **End session** ends it early (after a confirmation); the time so far counts.
- When the time is up, a notification arrives on time and a **short break**
  begins – after every fourth session a **long break**. **Skip** returns home.
- Change the length right on home: **tap the duration chip** and turn the
  wheel (5-minute steps). The value is saved – the settings show the same.
- Break lengths and the long-break rhythm: *Settings → Focus / Short break /
  Long break / Long break after*.

## Daily goal

The ring fills with the focus time of today's sessions. **Hold the ring** to set
the daily goal with a wheel (10 minutes to 12 hours); the settings have it too,
with + / − for small steps.

## Labels and goals

- **Tap the label chip** to choose what the next session is about, or
  *Manage labels* to add, rename and delete labels.
- A label can have a **total goal** (e.g. 20 h for an exam) and/or a **weekly
  goal** (e.g. 5 h per week, Monday to Sunday). Both are optional.
- The label then shows e.g. “12h of 20h” and “3h of 5h this week” with thin
  progress lines; reached goals say so and keep counting. Totals count all
  sessions of the label, also those before the goal was set.

## Session ritual

- **Intention:** type what you're working on before you start; it shows during
  the session and in the statistics.
- **Reflection:** after a completed session, the break asks “How did it go?” –
  Heavy, Steady or Flowing.
- **Sound:** tap the sound chip – Rain, Soft noise, Deep noise, Fireplace, Ocean
  waves (with volume), or **Your music app**, which plays and pauses your last
  music or podcast app together with the session.

## Pausing apps

1. Tap the **paused apps chip** and choose the apps that should rest while you
   focus.
2. Tap **Allow app blocking**: Focus explains what the accessibility service
   does. Tap **Agree and open settings** and switch on **Focus app pause**.
3. Tap **Allow holding notifications** and switch on **Focus notification hold**.

During a session a paused app doesn't open: Focus shows a calm screen
(“Instagram is resting while you focus”) – **Return to focus** brings you back.
Notifications of paused apps wait and arrive when the session ends.
Blocking is strict on purpose: the list can't be shortened during a session.

**Block lists:** besides your default list you can create named lists (e.g.
“Morning”) in the paused apps screen and use them for focus times.

## Focus times

*Settings → Focus times*: block apps automatically on fixed days and hours,
e.g. Monday–Friday 7–10. One focus time can hold several time ranges with
their own days and hours, and use your default list or a named list. When a focus time begins, a
reminder appears – tapping it starts a session. Focus times count in
the statistics as protected time, not towards your daily goal.

## Mindful opening

*Settings → Mindful opening*: outside sessions and focus times, opening a
paused app first shows a short breathing pause (3, 5, 10 or 20 s, default 5);
then you choose **Open …** (e.g. “Open Instagram”) or **Go back**. An opened app stays free while you use
it and 10 more minutes after leaving it. The page has its own app list.

## Widget, tile and lock screen

- **Widget:** add the Focus widget to your home screen – one tap starts a
  session with your defaults without opening the app; while running it shows
  the countdown.
- **Quick settings tile:** edit your quick settings and add **Focus**. A tap
  starts a session; while it runs the tile shows the end time, a tap opens
  Focus. Long press opens Focus.
- **Lock screen:** the countdown with label shows on the lock screen (a Live
  Update on Android 16). Switch it off in *Settings → Show on lock screen*.

## Statistics

Tap the statistics icon on home:
- **Today** and **this week / month**, switchable with Week / Month.
- **Day bars:** tap a day to see its focus time against the goal and its
  sessions with intention and reflection.
- **Streak:** current and best run of days reaching the daily goal.
- **Insights:** how long your paused apps rested and on how many days you
  focused.
- **Paused apps:** each app's rest time, split into focus times and sessions.
- **By label** for the period and **All time** with goals and weekly progress.

## Settings

- **Background:** Five stems, Dense branches, Roots (default), Two trees, Fine
  mesh or None – with live previews. Android's “Remove animations” keeps
  everything still.
- **Language:** System, English, Deutsch, Русский.
- **Backup:** *Export backup* saves a file where you choose; *Restore backup*
  reads it (not possible during a session or focus time).

## FAQ

**The switch for “Focus app pause” is greyed out.** See
[Restricted settings](#install).

**Paused apps still open.** Check that *Focus app pause* is on (Focus warns on
home and with a notification when it's off) and that a session or focus time is
running.

**Will I miss notifications?** No – held notifications arrive as soon as the
session or focus time ends.

**Does Focus send my data anywhere?** No. Focus has no internet access; see the
[privacy policy](store/privacy-policy.md).
