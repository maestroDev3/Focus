# Store listing – English

**App name (≤ 30):** Focus – Calm Focus Timer

**Short description (≤ 80):** A calm Pomodoro timer that pauses distracting apps while you focus.

**Full description:**

Focus is a calm, minimal focus timer. Tap the ring and your session begins.

• Pomodoro rhythm – focus sessions with short and long breaks
• Pause distracting apps – chosen apps stay closed while you focus, their notifications wait until you're done
• Focus times – block apps on fixed days and hours, e.g. weekdays 7–10
• Mindful opening – a short breathing pause before opening a paused app outside your focus
• Labels with goals – a total and a weekly goal per topic, e.g. “Uni · 5h this week”
• Honest statistics – per day, week and month, by label, protected time and paused apps
• Session ritual – an intention before, a short reflection after, calm ambient sounds
• Home screen widget, quick settings tile and lock screen countdown
• A calm, living design in Noir & Champagne with backgrounds to choose from
• English, Deutsch, Русский

No account, no ads, no tracking: all your data stays on your phone.

Focus uses Android's accessibility service only to notice which app is opened, so it can bring you back when a paused app is opened during your focus. It never reads your screen.
